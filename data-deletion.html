PixiflixPrivacy Policy

Data Deletion

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Pixiflix does not intentionally collect personal information from players through the normal operation of our games.

Some games may store game-related information needed for gameplay, such as progress, settings, or other game state. To ask about deletion of game-related data, contact pixiflixstudio@gmail.com.

In your request, identify the game and explain what data you want deleted. Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Third-party services

If data is held by a third-party platform or service rather than by Pixiflix, deletion may need to be requested from that provider under its own privacy policy and procedures.

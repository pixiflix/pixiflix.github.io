Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixiflix ("Pixiflix", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in our games available on Android, iOS, and PC.

Our current approach: Pixiflix does not intentionally collect personal information from players as part of the normal operation of our games. Some non-personal or game-related data may be processed when it is necessary to provide, save, secure, or improve game functionality.

1. Information we use

Depending on the game and features you use, we may process information necessary for the game to work, such as game progress, settings, gameplay state, technical information, or other data required by the game itself.

2. How we use game-related data

Game-related data may be used as needed to operate and support the game, including saving progress, providing game features, preventing abuse or cheating, troubleshooting problems, and maintaining game performance.

3. Personal information

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your real-world name, home address, or government identification through the normal operation of our games.

4. Third-party services

Some games may use platform or third-party services for distribution, achievements, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, payments, or advertising. Where such services are used, those providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Children's privacy

Our games are not designed to require players to provide personal information to Pixiflix. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through the normal operation of our games.

6. Data retention

Game-related data may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide relevant game features, maintain game integrity, or satisfy technical and legal requirements.

7. Data deletion

For deletion requests, see our Data Deletion page.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when our games, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact Pixiflix at pixiflixstudio@gmail.com.

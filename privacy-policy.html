Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how Pixiflix ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Urvi Adventure for Android and when you contact us. The game's Android package name is com.PixiFlix.UrviAdventure.

1. Information used

The game does not ask you to enter your real name, home address or government identification to play. However, the game and its service providers may process device and advertising information that can qualify as personal data.

  • Game progress and preferences: level unlocks, gameplay state, audio settings and controller positions and sizes are stored on your device.
  • Device-related information: the game uses a device-related identifier or a generated identifier for local save handling, and records first-launch information and app-open counts.
  • Advertising data: depending on the provider, configuration and applicable privacy choices, advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and operating-system details, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information.
  • Support: when you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message.

2. How information is used

Information is used to operate the game, remember progress and preferences, provide and measure advertising, maintain security and performance, investigate technical problems and respond to support or privacy requests. The information processed depends on the features used and the relevant service provider.

3. Advertising and third-party services

Urvi Adventure integrates Unity LevelPlay for ad mediation, with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads as configured ad networks. Advertising includes interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos. These services may process information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Their privacy notices explain their practices and available choices:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy — for Google Play and Google-hosted services.

The game checks internet access using HTTPS requests to Google-hosted connectivity endpoints. These requests expose network information such as your IP address to the destination and include a random request token to avoid cached responses. The connectivity check does not send your gameplay save.

The rating button opens Google Play, which handles ratings and reviews. Social buttons open external websites or apps, whose privacy policies apply when you use them. The share button uses your device's sharing interface to share the game link.

4. Privacy choices

Android provides advertising-ID and other privacy controls, depending on your device and operating-system version. These settings may limit certain advertising uses but do not necessarily stop all advertising or data processing.

You can contact us with privacy requests at pixiflixstudio@gmail.com. For information processed independently by advertising or platform providers, their privacy notices describe additional choices and request channels.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or opt out of certain sale, sharing or targeted-advertising activities. Rights are subject to applicable conditions and exceptions. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

5. Younger players

Urvi Adventure is intended for players aged 13 and older. This does not mean all players are adults; privacy and consent requirements for teenagers vary by location. If you believe a child has provided information that requires special handling, please contact us.

6. Storage, retention and security

Local game progress and preferences remain on your device until removed or overwritten. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local data, although device backup and restore settings may affect this. Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their policies and applicable obligations.

Support information is retained as needed to respond to requests and address relevant legal or security requirements. Providers may process information outside your country; their privacy notices explain relevant international processing and safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Deletion requests

To request deletion of information held by Pixiflix, email pixiflixstudio@gmail.com or visit our Data Deletion page. We may need reasonable information to identify relevant records and verify your request. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Removing the app does not automatically delete information held independently by advertising providers or Google Play. You may need to use their privacy request channels for those records.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy as the game, its services or applicable requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Additional notices or choices will be provided where required.

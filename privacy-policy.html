This policy explains how Pixiflix ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Urvi Adventure for Android, package com.PixiFlix.UrviAdventure, and when you contact us about the game. Other Pixiflix games and platforms may use different features or providers; do not assume the integrations described here apply to all of them.

Our Android games are listed on our Google Play developer profile. For questions about this policy, email pixiflixstudio@gmail.com.

1. Information processed by the game

Urvi Adventure does not require you to enter a real-world name, home address or government identification to play. This does not mean that the game or its service providers process no personal data. Device identifiers, IP addresses and advertising information may be personal data under applicable law.

  • Progress and preferences: level unlocks, recent level, gameplay state, audio settings, controller layout and other preferences are stored on your device to support gameplay.
  • Device-related information: the inspected game code obtains a device-related identifier, or generates an identifier when one is unavailable, and records information such as first-launch date and app-open count. Device-related information is also used in local save handling.
  • Advertising information: depending on SDK settings, availability, applicable law and your choices, advertising providers may process advertising or app/device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and operating-system details, app information, ad impressions, interactions, diagnostic information and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Support communications: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, government IDs or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. How information is used

Information is used to operate the game, remember progress and settings, deliver and measure advertising where permitted, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, respond to requests and meet applicable obligations. The applicable lawful basis depends on the processing and jurisdiction. Where consent is required, processing must follow a valid consent decision; other processing may require a different lawful basis.

Publisher review required: confirm and document the lawful basis for each actual processing activity before release. This draft does not establish that legitimate interest or another basis is available for every activity.

These providers and their relevant advertising partners process information to supply, measure and protect advertising according to their services, settings and privacy notices.

5. Internet checks and external services

The game requires a working internet connection from the level-selection screen onward. Its connectivity check makes HTTPS requests to Google-hosted connectivity endpoints. The destination receives network information such as the source IP address; each check also includes a random request token to avoid cached responses. These checks are separate from ad consent and do not send an ad reward or a gameplay save as part of the connectivity request.

The rating button opens the game's Google Play listing. Google Play handles any rating or review you submit. The game does not receive confirmation of whether you submitted a review. Social buttons open external services such as YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, whose policies apply when you use them. Sharing opens your device's sharing interface; the app does not need access to your contact list to offer the link.

Publisher review required: the project also contains licensing/integrity and backend-related components. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal or security needs.

Providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy notices describe relevant processing locations and safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Publisher review required: confirm actual retention periods or criteria for any backend logs, integrity requests and other server-side records, and the relevant international-transfer safeguards. Do not claim fixed deletion deadlines or storage locations without verification.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising-related processing. Some rights have exceptions. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Contact us at pixiflixstudio@gmail.com or see our Data Deletion page. We may need reasonable information to locate relevant records and verify a request. Do not send government identification unless specifically necessary and securely requested.
